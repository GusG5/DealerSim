# DealerSim v3.1 — Calibration Lab

## Purpose

The calibration lab is a deterministic regression suite for simulator incentives. It asks a practical question: can an unsophisticated fixed strategy achieve an implausibly strong process score simply by exploiting the scoring or execution rules?

The v3.1 standard pass uses 32 seeds for each of 12 policies, giving 384 scripted sessions.

## Defect found during v3.1 development

The first lab run found that an all-cash fund could receive an overall score of roughly 77 despite taking essentially no mandate risk. That was a scoring exploit: strong risk control was being rewarded without enough penalty for inactivity.

The fund score now applies an inactivity penalty for extremely low turnover / gross exposure. The same all-cash policy now averages 48.8 in the broader 32-seed calibration pass.

## Why fixed policies are useful

These strategies are intentionally unsophisticated. A good simulator should not allow one of them to dominate simply because of a structural loophole such as:

- commission overwhelming risk penalties;
- immediate hedging being effectively free;
- never trading receiving a top portfolio score;
- one execution venue always beating every alternative;
- maximum beta exposure being rewarded as alpha.

The lab should be extended whenever a new execution mechanic or scoring component is introduced.
